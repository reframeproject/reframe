Who are we?

Reframe is an independent, international digital platform where people from diverse cultures, languages, and nationalities come together to break down walls of social prejudice. Our goal is to create a comfortable environment where everyone can freely express their ideas and thoughts and openly share their culture.

Everyone has a memory to tell and an experience to share. At Reframe, we value these and aim to create a safe environment where everyone can share them.

We are working together to eliminate societal judgments and pressures on this path, where we aim for people to approach one another with tolerance and without judgment.
